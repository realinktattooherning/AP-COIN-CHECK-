Radar

      
Every 2 minutes the radar pulls new Solana coins from DexScreener (new profiles, boosts, takeovers) and GeckoTerminal (new and trending pools). Coins that pass the screener (graduated, market cap over $50K, under a day old, more volume than market cap, not more sells than buys) get the full scan. Only coins scoring 6/10 or more are listed. Each shows when it was scanned, so you know how fresh the score is. The list starts over every 24 hours.

          The rulebook
          
        
          24 checks.
          One score.
          
        
          Every rug leaves fingerprints: bundled wallets, fake volume, a dev who can still mint. These are the ones DK Rug Scan looks for. Rules marked hard no cap the score at 2 on their own.
          
      
          
      
          
        
          
          
          Who holds it
          
          
          A handful of wallets that can dump on you is the most common rug of all.
          
          
            
            
          • V14Top 10 holders own over 30%hard no
            Pools and known accounts are excluded.
            •

            Bundles buy near-equal bags — 1.48%, 1.47%, 1.47%, 1.47%.
            • 
            
          • V18Farmed walletshard no
            Top holders whose wallets were all created on the same day, often months earlier.
            • 
            
          • V8Insider networks
            Wallets linked by transfers, holding a big share of supply.
            • 
            
          • DEVDev holds over 5%
            The creator's own bag.

          Can they pull it
          
          
          Contract powers and liquidity decide whether the rug can be pulled at all.
          
          
            
            
          • MINTMint authority still onhard no

            The dev can freeze your tokens.

          Questions
          
        
          FAQ
          
        
          Is it really free?
          Yes. No sign-up, no wallet, no limits. It runs in your browser and reads public data from DexScreener, Rugcheck, GeckoTerminal, pump.fun, Solana RPCs, honeypot.is and GoPlus.
          
        
          Which chains does it scan?
          Solana gets all 24 checks. Ethereum, Base, BSC, Arbitrum and other EVM chains get honeypot, sell-tax and contract-power checks plus the market and holder rules.
          
        
          Does a high score mean it will pump?
          No. A high score means none of the automated rug signs showed up. You still judge the meme, the community and the team yourself — the report lists what to check.
          
        
          Why does a report say INCOMPLETE?
          One of the data sources didn't answer, or the coin is only minutes old. The score is capped at 3 until every check has run. Scan again in a minute.
          
        
          Does it work on my phone?
          Yes. Open this page, paste the address and tap Scan coin.
